One camera · Several cameras

webhands · cameras

Any number of cameras: this computer's, and phones joining over WebRTC. The first camera is the reference. Put cameras 20–60 cm apart, all seeing where your hands will be. Turn off Center Stage (macOS Video Effects): it changes the camera geometry.

Cameras on this computer

Phones

Scan with a phone on the same network to add it as a camera.

1 · Each camera's field of view

Edit the FOV on each camera tile, or click "A4" there and click the corners of a sheet of A4 tilted 30–60° (more accurate; phones do this on their own screen). Keep the sheet flat: lay it on a book or clipboard — a 2 cm bow costs ~3–6 %.

2 · Hand size (scale)

Hold a credit card by its long edge between thumb tip and index fingertip, facing the first camera.

3 · Camera positions

Hold up one open hand where the cameras see it (best keep the other hand out of view). Move it slowly through the whole shared space for 12 s: near and far, side to side, turning it. Also measures your hand's bone lengths.

Debugging

Recordings contain each camera's hand landmarks, timing and calibration (no images). Calibration runs are recorded automatically.
