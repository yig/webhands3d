One camera · Two cameras

webhands · stereo

Two cameras in one tab: e.g. the laptop camera and an iPhone via Continuity Camera. Put them 20–50 cm apart, both seeing where your hands will be. Turn off Center Stage (Control Center → Video Effects): it crops and zooms, which changes the camera geometry.

1 · Each camera

Field of view per camera, or click a tilted sheet of paper's corners in that camera's view (more accurate; add a few views).

2 · Hand size (scale)

Hold a credit card by its long edge between thumb tip and index fingertip, facing camera A.

3 · Camera pair

Hold up one open hand where both cameras see it. Move it slowly through the whole shared space for 12 s: near and far, left and right, up and down, turning it.
